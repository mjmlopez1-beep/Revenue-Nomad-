## Section 21: Faq

COMMON QUESTIONS
### H2 What companies ask first
FAQ Q What is a fractional revenue leader?
A senior sales, marketing or RevOps executive who runs the function 1 to 3 days a week - on your leadership team, accountable for a number, at a fraction of a full-time cost.
FAQ Q What does a fractional leader cost?
$4k - $27k/month depending on the seat and days per week, from our 2026 data. No fee on top - you pay the operator's rate. See the Rate Index
FAQ Q How fast can this start?
We speak within 24 hours and you meet matched leaders within days - not the two quarters a full-time search takes, and with no six-month onboarding after.
FAQ Q How is this different from consulting?
Consultants advise from the sidelines. A Revenue Nomad operator sits in your org chart, goes to the board meeting and owns the result - with the scoped rigor we kept from our consulting roots.
