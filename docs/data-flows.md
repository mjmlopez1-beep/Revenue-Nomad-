# Where Merlin's data goes

The same content as the public page at `/data` (`app/data/DataFlows.tsx`), with the code it was checked against and the points the owner still has to confirm. Keep the two in step. Lines marked **To confirm** are for the owner or a lawyer and are not on the public page.

## 1. When an AI model is used

- Merlin's server calls no AI model, except when Merlin drafts a proposal for a member (`lib/proposals/draft.ts`, in the app "Draft the whole proposal with Merlin"; Anthropic is the AI service provider, and its API terms don't let it train on what is sent), which is off unless `RN_AI_DRAFTS=1`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `RN_AI_MODEL` are all set (`lib/proposals/aiConfig.ts`), and which a member agrees to before their first draft (`State.aiDrafts`). Then one request to Anthropic's Messages API carries that draft's inputs: the transcript or notes the member pasted or picked from their Granola (part 3), the template, the company's facts as the member's table shows them, the rate range from the benchmarks, the member's name, title and writing voice. The transcript is used for that request only and never stored or logged; only the draft is saved. It is on Close and the trial (`lib/billing/rules.ts` `canClose`), metered against each member's monthly AI usage (`lib/proposals/aiUsage.ts`): for each call Merlin keeps only how many tokens went in and out and what that cost, as numbers (`State.aiUsage`, the last 200 calls), never a word of the call or the draft. The member sees a percentage of the month's AI usage; the owner sees the month's totals in Pilot metrics.
- Changing a proposal by asking (`lib/proposals/change.ts`, "Change it" in the editor and the connector's `change_proposal`) runs behind the same switch, the same agreement and the same AI usage: one request to Anthropic's Messages API carries the proposal's sections and fields, the lines the member kept from the call, their writing voice and the instruction they typed or said. No transcript. Nothing is saved until the member accepts; then the version before is kept on the proposal (`StoredProposal.versions`, up to 20, the member's alone like the proposal) so they can compare it or bring it back. Speech for the mic is the browser's own recognition: Merlin receives only the words it typed into the box. Without the switch, the connector's `change_proposal` takes sections the member's own Claude rewrote and Merlin only checks the numbers (`lib/proposals/numbers.ts`, rules) and saves them.
- Starting a proposal from a call (`app/post/ProposalStart.tsx`): a pasted or dropped transcript, a `.docx` included (read in the browser, `lib/proposals/startCall.ts` `docxText`), and what the member says was agreed are read in the browser by Merlin's rules (`lib/proposals/extract.ts`, `readTerms`); only the lines found and the terms read are sent and kept. The connector's `draft_proposal_from_call` takes a transcript the member's own Claude already fetched (from Granola or anywhere) and reads it the same way on Merlin's server, for that call only: it is never stored or logged, and only the lines found are kept with the draft.
- Drafting and changes, live (`lib/proposals/live.ts`, `app/api/proposals/live`): while Merlin writes, the steps and the text written so far are held in the server's memory for that member alone, for 15 minutes after it ends, so the app can pick up after a dropped connection. Never the transcript, never on disk. A cancelled draft saves nothing; a stream that drops keeps the sections written whole as a draft.
- A client added from its website (`lib/proposals/addCompany.ts`): Merlin reads that site's homepage once (robots.txt first, a 3.5-second limit, as `lib/about` reads homepages) for the name it gives itself, and adds the company through the ordinary Add a company action. Other members see the company like any other a member adds, never who added it or that there is a proposal. At draft time the company is looked up by its name or the website the call gives (the signer's email domain, never a shared mail host), the way `find_company` finds one.
- Contracts dropped in for signature (`lib/documents/smart.ts`, `lib/documents/detect.ts`): Merlin reads the uploaded PDF's text and where it sits on the page (pdf.js, on its own server, no model) to find the signature lines, and looks up the other party's company by its name the way `find_company` does, using the member's own view of its decision maker. Nothing from the reading is stored except the fields, signers, title and note the member keeps; the PDF stays on the data volume as before.
- **To confirm:** Anthropic's commercial terms and data retention for the API key Revenue Nomad uses, before turning `RN_AI_DRAFTS` on.
- AI comes in only when a member adds Merlin to their own assistant (Claude, ChatGPT) with the connector (`app/api/mcp`, `lib/mcp`). The assistant calls Merlin's tools as that member, with a token that member granted (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, `lib/mcp/oauth.ts`). Each tool answers from the member's own view (`lib/mcp/view.ts`, `lib/mcp/tools.ts`): the same locks, credits and limits as the app. Nothing is sent to the assistant that the member couldn't see in the app.
- What the assistant's provider does with those answers is governed by the member's own account with that provider.
- The member sees and disconnects connected assistants in Your credits, Connected apps (`app/post/ConnectedApps.tsx`, `disconnectApp` in `lib/mcp/oauth.ts`). An assistant can also revoke its own grant (`/api/oauth/revoke`).

## 2. Does any of it train a third party's model?

- Merlin sends no data to model providers, so nothing from Merlin goes into their training.
- Google user data (`lib/google`) is used under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: never sold, never used for ads, never used to train AI models, used only to show a member their own network, never shown to another member or an admin, never added to shared counts or Crystal Ball's model (`lib/google/types.ts`).
- Merlin never reads email text or meeting titles:
  - Calendar events are requested with a `fields` mask that leaves out the title (summary), description, location and attachments (`lib/google/api.ts` `EVENT_FIELDS`, `calendarEvents`).
  - Gmail is read with `format=metadata` and only the From, To, Cc, Date and bulk-mail headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Id, Precedence, Auto-Submitted), under the `gmail.metadata` scope, which cannot read bodies (`lib/google/api.ts` `HEADERS`, `lib/google/config.ts` `SCOPE_GMAIL`).
  - Contacts: names, email addresses, organization and job title only (`personFields` / `readMask` in `contacts`).
- **To confirm:** Google's OAuth verification status for the scopes in `MAIN_SCOPES` and `gmail.metadata` (Gmail is offered only to listed testers today, `RN_GOOGLE_GMAIL_TESTERS`), and whether a Limited Use disclosure is also needed in a privacy policy on revenuenomad.com.
- The Privacy Policy (`/privacy`, `app/privacy/Privacy.tsx`) and Terms of Service (`/terms`, `app/terms/Terms.tsx`) sit beside this page. The Google verification kit, with what the owner still has to confirm for them, is `docs/google-verification.md`.

## 4. Inside Merlin: Peers

- Peers (`lib/peers.ts`, `lib/peersCore.ts`, `State.peers`) keeps the leads members pass to each other, the team-ups they start and the circles they join: who, which company, and what they wrote (a pass's note, a team's draft, a circle's posts). Each is shown only to the members in it: a pass to its two members, a team to its two, a circle's posts to its members.
- None of it goes to an outside service, except the Peers notices by email (part 3). The admin export keeps who and which company and leaves out what members wrote (`lib/table.ts` `exportState`).
- Names in Peers follow the Fractionals directory (`lib/fractionals.ts`): a member who said no to the directory is left out, and names taken off with Remove my name, outside the US or unverified are not on file.
- Who sees a member's name on their seats (`lib/tableFields.ts` `seatNameWho`, from `PEERS_FREE`): fellow fractionals see it free once they can see the company; in the directory, every member sees it free; out of the directory, it costs 1 credit once the company is open.
- Hide my name on my seats (`Profile.nameHidden`, listed in `State.namesHidden`; Your profile, Your seats, onboarding, Home's directory question): every seat of theirs reads "A fractional (name hidden)" (`NAME_HIDDEN`) to every other member, signed out, in the connector and in exports of the view (`lib/table.ts` `cellOf`, `holderNameHidden`). The member's own view keeps their name. Their seats and roles still count; the role opens with the company as a fellow fractional's does. They are out of the Fractionals directory, never named in Your way in (`lib/paths`), and no conversation can start through their seat (`lib/messages.ts`).

### Who you know: the member's own sources

- Three sources, each on its own (the owner's decision): the member's Google contacts and calendar (`lib/google`, only while `RN_GOOGLE=1`), their own LinkedIn connections file, and any contacts CSV they bring in (`lib/paths`). The two files need no Google. Each alone gives People I know and ways in; together they merge into one entry a person (`lib/paths/index.ts` `contactsOf`: by email, or by name at the same company). They are brought in from People ("Bring in who you know", `app/post/network/ConnectPanel.tsx`) or Your network.
- The contacts CSV (`State.paths[memberId].csv`, `lib/paths/csv.ts`): the page reads the file in the browser, guesses which columns are the name, company, title and email, and shows the member a preview; the member can change the guess. Only those four columns are sent (`pickColumns` writes a new, smaller CSV in the browser): no other column of the file ever reaches Merlin. The server reads it again (`parseContacts`) and keeps, for each person with a name and a company or an email, the name, company, title and email, and when the file was read and how many rows were skipped (a count). Never the file itself. The two files share the daily limit of 3 uploads.
- A match at a locked company (the owner's default, `lib/paths/types.ts` `fromFile`, `lockedMatchesHidden`): what comes from the LinkedIn file or a contacts CSV never says which locked company it matched. People I know, the person bubble, ways in, Your hidden paths, Where you can get in, Home and the connector (`pathsLine`, `accountPathsLine`, `ways_in`) give such a company as a count only ("You know someone at 4 locked companies. Open them to see who.", `KnownView.locked`, `PathsView.lockedWays`); the company and the person are named once the member opens it. Google's network names the companies it reads from the member's own mail and calendar, as before. `RN_LOCKED_MATCHES=show` brings back the earlier behaviour.
- Not a relationship (`State.paths[memberId].away`): the member's own keys for people they set aside, nothing else. They leave the member's ways in and show under Set aside, with Undo.
- People I know (`GET /api/paths?known=1`, `lib/paths/index.ts` `knownView`) is worked out when asked for and never stored: the member's own words for each person (their name, title and email as the member's own file, contacts or messages give them), how warm (Google or messages only), and the last touch as a date (never what was said). It says which person record on Merlin is the same person only where the match is precise (the same public profile, or the one record with the same name at the same company) and only at a company the member can see; that record keeps its locks and prices.
- All of it is the member's alone, at the same level as their Google network: never shown to another member or an admin, never in the admin export (`lib/table.ts` `exportState` leaves `State.paths` out), never used for anyone else's results, and never named by the connector (counts only).
- Deleting: Delete my contacts file (People or Your network, `POST /api/paths { op: "deleteCsv" }`, `lib/paths/index.ts` `deleteContacts`) deletes the rows and every plan for a way in through them (`lib/paths/plan.ts` `forgetSource`); the LinkedIn file has its own Delete. Closing an account deletes both files and the set-aside list with the member's personal data 30 days on (`lib/table.ts` `purgeMember` deletes `State.paths[memberId]`).
- `RN_PATHS=off` switches the files, People I know and ways in off together (`lib/paths/index.ts` `pathsOn`).

### Suggested additions from your connections (`RN_SUGGEST`, off by default)

- What it does (`lib/paths/suggest.ts`, Warm paths): after a member brings in their LinkedIn connections file or a contacts CSV, Merlin lists the people in it whose title reads as one of the company's own leaders (C-level, VP, Head of, founder, owner, president, managing director or partner, general manager; never a fractional, interim, adviser or consultant title) at a company on Merlin with no decision maker or leader on file, matched precisely (the email's work domain, else the company's exact name, as People I know matches). Worked out when asked for, from the member's own files; never stored as a list.
- What is added: only the people the member ticks, one member add each (`lib/table.ts` action `suggestAdd`, `POST /api/paths { op: "suggestAdd", keys }`). The server works the list out again from the member's files and adds only rows whose key it finds there; nothing from the page is trusted. Each person goes in with their name, title and the relationship Decision maker only: never an email, a phone, a profile address or any other field from the file. The record is marked `via: "suggest"` (`State.contacts`). The name earns 1 credit, inside the daily earning cap (25) and the daily fact limit; the title and the relationship earn nothing. Nothing is added without the tick.
- Who is never suggested: anyone from Google (Google's contacts and calendar stay private to the member, Google's Limited Use rules); anyone at a company outside the United States; anyone in California: the two files hold no person's location, so Merlin leaves out every company based in California too, and no Californian comes in through here (`lib/california.ts`); a name that isn't a person's (one word, initials only, a greeting or a page's words, `lib/personName.ts`); the member themself and other members; anyone at a company that already has a leader or the same name on file; anyone who asked to be removed there (Remove my name's mark, `State.leadersRemoved`); anyone the member said no to. A match at a company still locked for the member is a count only (the locked-match rule).
- Kept: the member's own "no" and "added" keys, one-way hashes of the company and the name (never a name), in `State.paths[memberId].suggest`: never in the admin export, deleted when the account's personal data is (`purgeMember` deletes `State.paths[memberId]`). The people added are member adds like any other: they stay on the record when the member closes their account, attributed to nobody.
- Removing: Remove my name (`/remove`, `lib/table.ts` `nameOffByName`) takes a suggested person off the company with every fact on them, refunds whoever paid to see them, and keeps a one-way mark so they are never suggested again.

### Your way in

- Your way into a company, Where you can get in, Warm paths (the new look's map, `GET /api/paths?warm=1`, `lib/paths/ways.ts` `warmOf`, `app/post/network/WarmPaths.tsx`), Home's move and the pipeline's route line (`lib/paths/ways.ts`, `app/post/network/Ways.tsx`) are worked out when asked for, from what Smart ways in already reads: the member's own Google network (counts and dates only, never an email's text or subject or a meeting's title), their own LinkedIn import and contacts CSV, when and how often they messaged members on Merlin (never what was said), and Merlin's shared seats, leaders and members. Each route's Est. success is a pure function of those (`lib/paths/rank.ts`).
- Before the member brings in anyone, Warm paths already has the routes Merlin sees on its own: a fellow fractional in the Fractionals directory (free to every member) who holds or held a seat at a company that fits, as a route to message them on Merlin. Never a fractional who keeps out of the directory or hides their name on their seats; at a company still locked for the member, the person is not named. Nothing about them is stored for this.
- When the member goes with a route, Merlin keeps their plan (`State.routes[memberId][companyId]`, `lib/paths/plan.ts`): the route's id and hop ids (hashes, never a name, an email or a profile address), when it was chosen, the step they are on, plan B, a log of what happened (the kind of event and the step, no text), the outcome of each step and the estimate when chosen. Names are worked out each time the plan is shown, by the naming rules that hold then. It goes on the company's deal in their pipeline (`PipelineEntry.route`): a new deal waits at To reach out, and moves to Sent when the first step's note or message goes out, by the pipeline's own rules (`lib/pipeline.ts` `routeEntry`, `openEntry`). The plan is the member's alone: never in another member's view, the shared table or the admin export (`lib/table.ts` `exportState`).
- The outcomes are also counted anonymously for calibrating the estimate (`State.routeStats`): how often each outcome happened, by the step's shape only (people in between, the link's word, whether the last person decides, the estimate). No member, company, person or route is in it, and it stays when an account closes. It goes to no outside service.
- Deleting: Disconnect and delete everything on Your network deletes every plan whose route went through someone from Google (`lib/google/index.ts` `forgetIn`); deleting the LinkedIn import deletes every plan through a connection from it (`lib/paths/index.ts` `deleteConnections`), and deleting the contacts CSV every plan through a contact from it (`deleteContacts`); closing an account deletes all of the member's plans when its personal data is deleted 30 days on (`lib/table.ts` `purgeMember` calls `lib/paths/plan.ts` `forgetMember`; until then a restore brings them back). A member can also drop a plan (`POST /api/paths { op: "way", do: "drop" }`); the deal stays in their pipeline.
- The connector's `ways_in` (`lib/mcp/ways.ts`, with ways in on, `RN_PATHS`: with or without Google, as the sources are independent) gives the routes with Est. success and steps, and never names a person from the member's own Google network, LinkedIn import or contacts CSV.

### Your notes on a company

- On a company's page (Notes and activity), a member can keep private notes on any company on file, open or locked, in their pipeline or not (`lib/companyNotes.ts`, `State.companyNotes[memberId][companyId]`): the text they typed (up to 2,000 characters a note, 100 notes a company), when they wrote it and when they last changed it. Added, changed and removed with the action `companyNote` (`lib/table.ts`), read with `GET /api/table?notes=<company id>`.
- Only the member who wrote them sees them: never in another member's view, the shared table, a connector's answer, an admin's panel, an email, a Slack post or the admin export (`lib/table.ts` `exportState` leaves the whole part out). Nothing in them goes to an outside service. A company removed from Merlin takes its notes with it, and two records of one company folded into one keep both sets.
- Deleting: remove a note from the Notes tab. Closing an account deletes all of them when its personal data is deleted 30 days on (`lib/table.ts` `purgeMember`).

### Crystal Ball: what counts most, and Why not

- Tuning (`lib/radar/tune.ts`, `Profile.radarTune`, action `radarTune`): for each group of signals (a role posted, a seat ended, a leader left or arrived, a fractional hire in another function, hired fractionals before, growing, no leader on the team page) Less, Normal or More. Only the groups off Normal are kept. It multiplies those signals' scores for this member alone (`lib/radar/match.ts` `scoreFor`), on the page and in their morning email; nobody else's ball changes, and with every group at Normal nothing is stored and every score is as before.
- Why not (`lib/radar/out.ts`, `State.radar.members[memberId].out`, action `radarOut`): a company the member took out of their ball, with the reason they picked (they have a full-time leader, too small or too big, wrong kind of company, I already know them, not now) and when, or one they asked to see anyway. Keyed by the internal company id, up to 500 a member. "They have a full-time leader" also saves the existing fact (`leaderReport`), as before. Nothing in it goes to an outside service or to another member. It is in Revenue Nomad's copy for admins with the rest of the member's Crystal Ball record.
- Deleting: Reset puts every weight back to Normal and removes `radarTune`; Bring it back removes an entry. Closing an account deletes both with the profile and Crystal Ball 30 days on (`lib/table.ts` `purgeMember`).

### Proposal readers (after a proposal or document goes out)

- What the client is told, near the foot of the proposal and the signing page (`app/sign/activity.tsx` `ActivityNotice`, `lib/close/types.ts` `disclosureText`): "<sender> sees when this is opened and which sections are read. Merlin counts this with a cookie on this page, never your IP address." No hidden tracking: the page sends nothing else.
- What is collected (`lib/close/activity.ts`, `POST /api/sign/activity`, signed out with a valid, live signing link only): that the page was opened; which section (a proposal's block by its own title on the client's page `app/sign/ProposalView.tsx`, a document's page) and which price option was on screen while the reader was active, in seconds, sent every 15 seconds; an option picked, an add-on turned on or off, a download. Each browser gets a random id in a cookie (`rn_reader`, HttpOnly, sent only to `/api/sign/activity`, 180 days), and Merlin keeps only an HMAC of it, to count readers (a forward opened in a new browser is a new reader). Never the IP address (it is used for the rate limit only and not kept), never a fingerprint. The device kind (phone, tablet or computer) comes from the browser's own user agent. The member who sent it, signed in, is not counted.
- Why: so the member knows when it was opened, by how many people, what was read, and when to follow up (`lib/close/rules.ts`, rules, no AI model).
- What is kept (`State.close.items`, by proposal or document id, `lib/close/types.ts` `CAPS`): per reader their visits and reading time; per section and option the time read, added up per reader; the last 60 visits, 20 downloads and 200 raw events. At most 20 readers, 60 sections and 24 options an item; a beacon's time is believed only up to the time since that reader's last one. Nothing is read once the link is signed, declined, withdrawn or expired, or while the member isn't on Close (`canClose`). After a signature only a download of the signed PDF is counted (`recordBeacon` with the signing link or the signed copy's own link, `app/sign/activity.tsx` `TrackedDownload`), and that page says so (`signedDisclosureText`: "<sender> sees when the signed PDF is downloaded from this page.").
- Who sees it: only the member who sent it (`GET /api/activity?id=`). Never another member, an admin's panel or the export (`lib/table.ts` `exportState`); account closure deletes it with the proposals (`purgeMember`).
- What leaves Merlin: the member's proposal alerts (first open, a new reader, time to follow up, a question the client asked, passed to someone else to sign, signed by the client with the member's countersignature to come, signed or declined, renewal; the proposal store's events reach them through `lib/proposals/store.ts` `setProposalHooks`, whose defaults are `lib/close/server.ts` `closeHooks`) to the member's own email (Resend) and Slack (if connected), in words, with no reader's id; reminders to the signer on day 2 (not opened), day 7 (not signed) and 2 days before the link closes, with the proposal's own email (`lib/close/reminders.ts`), each off per proposal or document: in the send sheets as it goes (`app/post/SendReminders.tsx`, `POST /api/activity { op: "reminders" }`) and on its activity panel after. A signing link works for 30 days unless the member picks 14, 60 or 90, or no end date (`expiresAt: null`, expiry `0` in the token; `lib/close/types.ts` `linkEnd`): a link with no end date never expires and gets no warning before it closes. Each alert can be switched per channel in Notifications (`State.close.prefs`).

### Signed copies and Check a copy

- A signed proposal sent on Close, and every completed document, gets a final PDF kept on the data volume (`lib/proposals/files.ts` at `RN_DATA_DIR/proposals/<send id>/final.pdf`, `lib/documents/files.ts`), never in the state, the repo, the export or the state's backup. It is emailed to both sides and downloaded only with the signed copy's link (`app/api/sign/pdf`, `app/api/documents/file`). It is deleted with the member's personal data (`lib/account.ts` purge).
- The seal (`lib/seal.ts`): the SHA-256 of the whole file and of the file with its printed seal line read as zeros, signed with Merlin's Ed25519 key, kept with the signed record (`StoredSend.seal`, `StoredDocument.completed.seal`). The key is `RN_SEAL_KEY` (PKCS#8 PEM); without it Merlin generates one once into the data folder (`seal-key.pem`, 0600, never in the repo) and logs that a generated key is in use. Its public half is at `GET /api/check`.
- A member's saved signature (`lib/proposals/memberSig.ts`, Close): typed words, or a drawing kept as a PNG on the data volume (`RN_DATA_DIR/members/<member id>/signature.png`, 0600) with its SHA-256 in the state (`State.memberSignatures`). Private to them: never in another member's view, the admin export or the repo. Each time it is applied (to a send as it goes out, or as a countersignature), a copy goes with that send (`proposals/<send id>/member-sig.png`) and the audit trail records when and how. Deleted by the member at any time, and with the account's personal data (`lib/account.ts`). On a document to sign, the sender signed in to Merlin can apply it in one click.
- On the client's proposal page: "Ask a question" sends the question by email to the member (reply-to the client's address; the member's address is never shown to the client), at most 5 a day on one link, and puts its first 300 characters on the send's audit trail. "Send to someone else to sign" sends a new link to the person the client names (name, title, email, an optional note); their name and email go on the proposal's audit trail and to the member by email. At most 3 hand-offs on one proposal (`lib/proposals/store.ts` `askQuestion`, `handOff`).
- `/check` (`app/check`, `POST /api/check`), public, no account: the PDF dropped there is read into memory, hashed and compared with the sealed records (`checkCopy`). It is never written to disk, logged, kept in the state or sent to an outside service, and the answer is only whether it matches and, if so, the signers' names and when they signed. Rate limited by IP like the signing page.
- **To confirm:** this is a hash and signature Merlin checks, not a certificate-based (PAdES) signature inside the PDF; whether a qualified or certificate-based signature is wanted for some clients.
