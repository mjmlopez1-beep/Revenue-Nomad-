questions, answered plainly

no legalese. no jargon.

Is this a loan?

No. A pre-settlement advance is a purchase of a portion of your future settlement proceeds. Because it’s non-recourse, you only repay if your case resolves in your favor. If it doesn’t, you keep the advance and owe nothing — which is why there’s no credit check and no monthly payment.

What does it cost?

Your offer letter shows a single fixed schedule — what you’d repay at settlement, with a hard cap of 2.0× the amount advanced. No compounding, no application fees, no processing fees, no surprises added later.

Will this affect my case?

No. We never direct strategy, we never contact the defense, and repayment comes out of settlement proceeds through your attorney at the end. Your lawyer stays fully in charge of your case.

How much can I get?

Advances typically range from $1,500 to $250,000, generally up to about 10–15% of a case’s conservatively estimated value — sized so your net recovery stays protected at settlement.

How fast is funding?

Most applicants get a decision within 24 hours of our call with their attorney, and funds within 24–48 hours of signing — by wire or check, your choice.

Does my attorney have to be involved?

Yes — and that protects you. We verify case details with your attorney and coordinate repayment through them at settlement, so you never juggle paperwork while you’re recovering.
