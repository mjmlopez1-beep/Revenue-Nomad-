about us

built on a simple promise.

Merlin Capital is a litigation finance firm. We advance money to plaintiffs while their cases are pending. The advances are non-recourse: if the case doesn’t win, they owe nothing. Everything else about how we work follows from taking that promise seriously.

why we exist

the antidote to an industry’s reputation

Litigation finance has a reputation problem: opaque pricing, compounding rates, and funders who treat injured people like line items. We founded Merlin Capital in 2026 to be the opposite: transparent, disciplined, and genuinely on the plaintiff’s side.

The premise is simple. A strong case shouldn’t be settled cheap because the plaintiff can’t pay rent while it’s pending. Our non-recourse advances level that field: if your case doesn’t win, you owe nothing. That’s not a technicality. It’s the entire promise.

“We built a litigation finance company that feels less like a bank and more like a trusted advisor who happens to be really good with money.”
