Attorneys don’t want a pitch. They want to know we understand liability, coverage, and duration, and that we will never compromise their client. Here’s how we work with the firms we serve.

the working relationship

what your clients get, and what we never do

what your clients get

  • Advances sized conservatively, typically 10 to 15% of case value, so net recovery stays protected.
  • A fixed repayment schedule with no compounding, and a contract fee disclosed up front.
  • One-page offers in plain English your clients actually understand.
  • Decisions within 24 hours of our underwriting call with you.
  • Staying power: patient capital means your client isn’t pressured into a lowball settlement.

what we never do

  • Touch case strategy. We purchase a receivable, not a voice in your case.
  • Contact opposing counsel, the court, or the defense. Ever.
  • Over-advance a case so the lien eats your client’s recovery.
  • Bury terms in fine print or renegotiate at settlement time.
  • Fund a case behind your back. Every advance runs through you.
