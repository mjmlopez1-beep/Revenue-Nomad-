# Merlin by Revenue Nomad

Merlin (formerly Trading Post) is a data exchange for fractional operators, run by Revenue Nomad for its members: they share what they know about fractional work to earn credits, and see which companies hire fractionals, what they've paid and which roles are open now. It records which companies hired fractionals, for which seat, who held each seat, what it paid, who the decision makers are, and which fractional roles are open now. Members add what they know (+1 credit a fact) and spend credits to see what other members added (1 credit a fact). A weekly scan of job boards keeps the open roles current.

## The product

The site *is* Merlin: `/` is the landing page, the login and the app (old `/table?k=` links redirect). Jigsaw for the fractional GTM community: every row is a fractional engagement — the company, the seat, and the people involved — and members **give** (add what's missing, verify what's right) to **get** (spend credits to unlock 🔒 fields).

- **Fill an empty field → +1 credit (up to 25 per rolling 24 hours). Unlock a 🔒 field someone else filled → 1 credit.** Starting balance: `RN_STARTER_CREDITS` (default 25, also set in `render.yaml` and the Dockerfile). When the starter balance goes down, anyone it would leave below 0 is lifted to 0 once, so nobody starts in debt. Invite bonuses (+10 each) and confirmation bonuses (+1) count toward the same 25; any part that doesn't fit is held and paid automatically as the rolling window frees room. New accounts can unlock 25 facts in their first 24 hours, then 100 a day.
- **Verify** any value you can see (once per person). Values show their confirmation count (✓n) even while locked. The count is members only: values from Revenue Nomad's own records or the scan carry no count and say where they came from (`Cell.from`: `records` or `posting`).
- **Report** a wrong value (members who have added a fact, or who paid to see the value, up to 10 a day): it is hidden for the reporter at once and for everyone after a second member reports it, until an admin (`RN_ADMIN_EMAILS`) reviews; removal reverses the filler's credit and refunds everyone who paid to unlock it. A seat holder's report on a fact on their own seat hides it for everyone at once. When an admin keeps a reported value, the members who reported it can't report that value again. Admins can also remove a fact a member added with no report (`adminRemove`), or everything one member added (`adminRemoveMember`: their facts, their benchmark-only rates and the empty records left behind), with the same reversals and refunds.
- **Server settings**: `RN_ID_SECRET` keys the public engagement and person ids (their internal ids contain names; generated by Render). Rate limits read the client address from the rightmost `X-Forwarded-For` entry; set `RN_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` (e.g. `true-client-ip`) or `RN_TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS` to match the host's proxy (`RN_LOG_IP_HEADERS=1` logs the candidate headers). `scripts/test-server.mjs` checks a running server end to end (`BASE=http://localhost:3000 STARTER=25 node scripts/test-server.mjs`, against a server on a fresh data dir started with `RN_SCAN_TEST=1 RN_CRON_SECRET=qa-cron`, which lets the tests store scan results without reading a job board; never set `RN_SCAN_TEST` in production). Three more files check a running server the same way, each on its own fresh data dir: `scripts/test-rules.mjs` (open roles, seats, reports, admin removal), `scripts/test-credits.mjs` (credit limits, invites, members) and `scripts/test-integration.mjs` (where those rules meet the scan and the benchmarks). Five checks need no server of their own: `npx tsx scripts/test-scan.mts` (the open-role collector, the scan's rules and the weekly email's wording, no network), `npx tsx scripts/test-digest.ts` (the weekly email run with a stubbed sender), `npx tsx scripts/test-model.mts` (the app's filters, free and locked lists and role dates), `npx tsx scripts/test-bench.mjs` (the rate benchmarks) and `npx tsx scripts/test-robust.mts` (see Staying up). `npm run test:all` runs every one of them after a build (`scripts/test-all.sh`, `PORT=4600` by default).
- **What a member sees first.** One next-move card at the top of the table: fill in your seats, then new open roles in your function this week, then low credits. It never shows with Get started, whose second step reads "Fill in your seats" for a member with seats on file. Dismissing a card hides that kind for 7 days.
- **Free and locked.** Free: company facts, function, type, start, why hired, pricing model, hours per week, revenue and employees at the time, a person's title and relationship, status, the dates of a role the scan follows, and the posting of a role the scan closed. Locked (1 credit each): role, seat holder, end, how sourced, rate, posting, and a person's name, email, phone and LinkedIn. A hidden company is one purchase with the role and posting of each open role there: asking for the company, such a role or such a posting buys all of them for 1 credit (one ledger row, on the company). Rate, seat holder and people stay separate.
- **Your seats.** The member a seat holder name resolves to sees every fact on their own seat free (`EngagementRec.yours`), can correct what Revenue Nomad's records or the scan say about it (no credit for a correction), and can say "This isn't me" (`notMine`) to unlink a wrong name match. On a name a member added, "This isn't me" also hides the name for everyone until an admin decides. People on the seat stay locked. The **Your seats** page (account menu) lists those seats with every missing fact answered in place: pick lists save in one tap, a rate goes on the record or to the benchmarks only, and "Add a seat we don't have" adds one the member held. Each fact earns 1 credit inside the daily 25.
- **Rate benchmarks** group by function and client revenue band. Points come from rates on records, rates members share for the benchmarks only (`benchRate`: +1 credit, counted in the medians, never shown with the member's name or the seat, left out of the admin export's member data; at most 5 saved a day and 3 that aren't tied to a seat the member holds), and Revenue Nomad's Rate Index (`rateIndex` in the seed). A figure counts people, not rows: a member counts once per group, with the middle of their rates. A group shows a median at 5 trusted contributors (Rate Index rows, our own records, seed members, accounts that joined through an emailed sign-in link, and accounts with a fact two trusted members confirmed), or at 20 when the figure includes Rate Index data, and the range (`p25`, `p75`) at 10 (`Benchmarks.rangeN`). Rates shared privately join a figure only with 5 of them in it, and rates from newer accounts only once it shows without them. Amounts are rounded to $1,000 a month and $25 an hour under 10 contributors, and to $250 and $5 from 10 on. A member who adds a rate to a seat's record after sharing one privately for that seat keeps one rate, on the record, and is paid once. The Rate Index points are anonymous (function, hourly rate, hours a month, client revenue band): `node scripts/add-rate-index.mjs` reads the git-ignored `data/rate-index.private.json` into the private seed, `scripts/build-intel-seed.py` carries them over on a rebuild, and `scripts/seal-intel-seed.mjs` re-seals the seed afterward.
- **Weekly scan.** Open fractional GTM roles come in with the day they were first seen; a role closes (status `Closed`, with the date) once two clean scans of its board, at least 12 hours apart, have missed it and it was last listed more than 10 days ago, and reopens if it is listed again. A scan of a board is clean only when the board answered, matched at least one role, and matched at least half of what it matched on its last clean run, so an outage or a reader that broke closes nothing (Pilot metrics lists each board in `admin.boards`). After the first clean miss a role is marked `unseen` and left out of the open count. When a role closes, unlocks of its role, posting and company made after it was last listed are paid back. An open role hides its company; closing shows it again. Only the member who took an open role can fill it, by picking themselves as its seat holder (once a day); any other name is refused. The role reopens if that name is removed or unlinked, and a posting that is still listed, or listed again, doesn't reopen a role a member has taken as their own (a name somebody else typed on a role the scan had closed doesn't outweigh the posting). Turn it on with `RN_SCAN=1` (a scan starts in the background when the last one is over 7 days old), or point a weekly cron at `POST /api/table/scan` with the header `x-cron-secret: $RN_CRON_SECRET` (the endpoint is off without `RN_CRON_SECRET`). Admins can also start one from the app (`scanNow`, the "Run scan now" button on Pilot metrics). The scan reads fractionaljobs.io, Remotive, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and the Greenhouse boards in `RN_GREENHOUSE_BOARDS` (`lib/scan/collect.ts`): listings only, GTM seats only, a real named company, posted in the last 60 days. Every posting that passes keeps its role open, and at most 60 roles are opened for the first time in one run. A board that fails is listed with its error on Pilot metrics and closes nothing. In the app, a followed role reads "Open since Nov 3" or "Role closed Dec 8 · listed 35 days", the Open roles filter has "Open now" and "Closed", and Hiring now says when the last scan ran. Run one scan on the host after the first deploy and read the per-source table: the collector has only been checked against saved pages, not the live boards.
- **Weekly email.** After a scan that found new roles, each member who has signed in gets the week's new open roles, unless they turned it off (`RN_DIGEST_ALL=1` sends it to every member on file with an email, signed in or not). It only sends when email is set up (`RESEND_API_KEY`, `RN_EMAIL_FROM`) and `RN_PUBLIC_URL` is set; `RN_DIGEST=0` keeps it off. Every email carries an unsubscribe link (`GET /api/table/unsubscribe`), and members can switch it in Your credits (`settings`). The switch shows only while the weekly email is on for the server (`View.scan.digest`). `RN_EMAIL_FOOTER` is the postal address line printed in the footer when set.
- **Pilot metrics** (admins, `View.admin`): members active in the last 7 and 30 days, credits at zero, how often members ran out of credits on an unlock, Pro waitlist interest and where it came from, the most unlocked and most added fields, scan and email health. It is in the account menu for admins, with "Download backup".
- **Staying up.** `GET /api/health` answers 200 only when the real member seed loaded, the state loaded and the data folder takes writes, else 503 with which part failed. Point the host's health check at it (`railway.json` does, with 15 seconds to drain). With `RN_REQUIRE_SEED=1` (set in the Dockerfile) the app never runs on the sample data: if the seed does not open, every route answers 503. A state file that cannot be read or parsed never starts as an empty table: the newest daily backup that parses is loaded, the damaged file is kept as `intel.json.corrupt-<time>`, and with no usable backup every route answers 503 and nothing is written until a good `intel.json` is put back. Only a missing file with an empty `backups/` folder is a first start. At most 4 table views are built at once and 50 wait; past that the answer is 503 with `Retry-After`. A request body over 64 KB is refused. `npx tsx scripts/test-robust.mts` checks all of this (it starts its own servers, so build first).
- **Speed.** A member's table is about 5.5 MB of JSON (a third of a megabyte compressed) and takes a few hundred milliseconds to build, so the page downloads it rarely. An action's answer carries what it changed (`viewPatch` in `lib/table.ts`: the records it touched as the member now sees them, the whole `Me`, the credit history rows it wrote, the scan summary, the benchmarks when a rate moved) and the table's version (`View.version`, `"<boot>:<commits>"`). The page applies the answer and downloads the table again only when the version says other members changed it too, and then a minute or so after its last download, or when the tab comes back after a minute away. Signed-out counts and the landing preview's companies are kept for 5 minutes whatever members change. A member's compressed table is kept while nothing changes (30 seconds at most). `node scripts/test-load.mjs` puts members to work at once and prints p50 and p90 of every kind of request (`LOAD_CLIENT=old` acts like the page before this, for a comparison).
- **Pro waitlist.** The pilot is free. Where members run out (the zero-credits popover, a hidden company's record, Your credits, the foot of Hiring now) a "Pro is coming" offer asks who wants more, with no price. The app records each view, each "Email me when it opens" and each tap on a lock at 0 credits (`signal`), and Pilot metrics reports them.
- **Add record** starts from the company URL: the server reads the site for the name and a suggested Revenue Nomad industry tag; the member sets revenue stage and employee count (Revenue Nomad pick lists, as they were at the time), the engagement, and the people (Reported to / Reported to me / Worked with). Everything is editable. **+ person** adds someone to any record.
- **Join** from the landing page with a first and last name + email (new people; a one-word name is refused, the same test a seat holder's name gets, so the member can be matched to their seats). Revenue Nomad members sign in with their personal link (or its code); a member's email can't be claimed by joining.
- **Undo.** A member can take back a fact they added (`retract`) while no other member has unlocked or confirmed it: the field opens again, the credit it earned comes off (ledger row "Took back the HQ"), and it no longer counts toward the day's 25. What the fact filled in on its own goes with it (the function read from a role, the "Ended" a dated end set). The fact that paid an invite bonus stays, and so does a correction of our records on the member's own seat.
- **What a seat can't say.** An open role nobody holds takes no start, end, why hired or how sourced ("This role is still open, so it has no start or end yet."); its rate and hours are the posted ones. On any seat an end can't come before the start or before the month the role was first listed, an Active seat takes no dated end, and a dated end on a seat with no status marks it Ended. A start or status is checked against a locked end only when the member can see that end, so a refusal never gives a locked fact away. A new seat needs a function or a role, and an Add that finds everything already on file says "Nothing new was added."
- **Companies and seat holders.** `Friendly.functions` counts hires only: an open role's function is in `Friendly.hiring` ("Hiring now"), and a posting that came down with no seat holder named counts in neither (it still scores like an open role). A seat a member added counts as fractional unless its type says otherwise. `CompanyRec.unlocked` marks a hidden company the member paid to see. A seat holder carries the "Member" tag (`cell.member`) only once that member has signed in, the same test as the seat-holder search. The source "Clay" reads "Revenue Nomad research".
- **Contact.** `View.contact` sends `RN_SUPPORT_EMAIL` (sign-in help, questions and feedback) and `RN_INTRO_EMAIL` ("Ask for an intro" to a seat holder), read when the server runs, to signed-out visitors too. Null when not set.
- Every row shows the company's icon (website favicon, else a background name lookup, else initials).
- **Limits beside the daily 25.** A company's pick lists (industry, employees, revenue and the four "How they sell" lists) earn a credit for 5 answers per member per rolling 24 hours and save for 20 (`Me.sellEarnLeft`), except on a company the member added or holds a seat at. A member can add 150 facts a day to records, not counting seats they hold. A new account can unlock 25 facts in its first 24 hours, the same as its starter credits, then 100 a day (`Me.unlocksLeft`). A balance below zero, after a fact was removed once its credit was spent, shows as 0 credits with the shortfall in `Me.owed`.
- **Invites.** Both members get 10 credits on the invitee's first fact on a record (a benchmark-only rate doesn't count). An inviter is paid for 5 different invitees per 30 days. An invite that joins from a network the inviter has signed in or visited from earns nothing (each member's last 5 networks are kept as hashes). Gmail addresses compare without dots and plus tags, so one inbox is one account.
- **Admins.** An address in `RN_ADMIN_EMAILS` has admin rights once it belongs to a member. While email sign-in is off it can't be claimed in the join form, so use a member's address, or create the account once by sending the join request with the header `x-cron-secret: <RN_CRON_SECRET>`. The seat-holder search lists only members who have signed in, 30 searches a minute. The admin export keeps sign-in codes (it is the backup), so treat the file like a password list.
- **Source** column/filter: *Published engagement*, *Member success story*, *Demand scan*, *Member added*.

Companies, engagements and contacts are linked entities (company 1→n engagements 1→n contacts): company data is entered and unlocked once for every row at that company. Fields and Revenue Nomad pick lists live in `lib/tableFields.ts`.

**Data.** `scripts/build-intel-seed.py` aggregates Revenue Nomad's own data: published engagements (admin portal export), every company named in Talent Signup success stories (the member's words become the record's context), Fractional GTM Demand Scan exports (`--demand`), and verified websites (`--domains`). It also writes one private sign-in link per member.

```bash
python3 scripts/build-intel-seed.py Talent_Signups.csv engagements.csv --demand demand-scan.csv --domains domains.json
node scripts/seal-intel-seed.mjs     # encrypts it to data/intel-seed.enc (key in data/intel-seed.key)
```

Member data never goes into git in the clear (this repo is public): `data/intel-seed.json`, `data/intel-member-links.csv`, `data/intel-seed.key` and runtime state are git-ignored. Hosts get the AES-256-GCM encrypted `data/intel-seed.enc` with the code and open it with `RN_INTEL_SEED_KEY` (the contents of `data/intel-seed.key`). Use persistent storage for `RN_DATA_DIR` (Railway volume / Render disk / Docker volume). With no seed, the page runs on labeled demo data (`/?k=demo`), unless `RN_REQUIRE_SEED=1` is set, as it is in the Docker image: then a seed that does not open means 503 on every route.

The weekly scan (`lib/scan`) is the only part that reads job boards. It uses the board readers, scoring and fetch in `lib/crawler` (`sources/`, `score.ts`, `fetch.ts`). The old job-board API (`/api/jobs`, `/api/crawl`, `/api/prospects`, `/api/profile`), its daily cron and its crawl workflow are gone: those paths answer 404.

## Launch / Deploy

Merlin keeps its state in one JSON file on a persistent disk (`RN_DATA_DIR`), held in memory by one Node process, so it needs a host with a volume:

- **Railway (production)**: `railway.json` builds the Dockerfile and points the health check at `/api/health`. Mount a volume at `/data`.
- **Render**: `render.yaml` provisions the web service plus a 1 GB persistent disk at `/var/data`, so Merlin's state survives restarts.
- **Docker (any host: Fly.io, a VPS)**: `docker build -t merlin . && docker run -p 3000:3000 -v merlin-data:/data merlin`. The image is a self-contained standalone build; mount a volume at `/data` to keep Merlin's state.

Serverless hosts with a throwaway filesystem (Vercel, Netlify) lose the state on every cold start, so they are not supported.

**The weekly scan:** set `RN_SCAN=1`, or point a weekly cron at `POST /api/table/scan` with the header `x-cron-secret: $RN_CRON_SECRET` (see Weekly scan above). CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) builds every push.

## Getting started

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:3000
```

Without the private seed the app runs on labeled demo data (`/?k=demo`). The weekly scan makes outbound HTTPS requests to the job boards above, so it needs normal internet access. Each board fails on its own: one being down never blocks the others.

## API

- `GET /api/table`, `POST /api/table`: the table as the signed-in member sees it, sign-in, joining and every action (see `app/api/table/route.ts` and `Action` in `lib/tableTypes.ts`). Signed out, `GET` answers counts only.
- `POST /api/table/scan`: the weekly scan, for a cron (`x-cron-secret`). `GET /api/table/unsubscribe`: the weekly email's unsubscribe link. `GET /api/table/lookup`: the Add form's prefill, members only.
- `GET /api/health`: 200 only when the seed opened, the state loaded and the data folder takes writes.
- `/api/mcp` and `/api/oauth/*`: the Claude connector (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE). `/api/integrations`: alerts in Slack and webhooks, members only.

## Configuration

See `.env.example`:

- `RN_GREENHOUSE_BOARDS`: comma-separated Greenhouse board tokens the weekly scan reads too
- `RN_DATA_DIR`: where Merlin's state lives (defaults to `./data`). Use a persistent volume
- `RN_STARTER_CREDITS` (default 25), `RN_ADMIN_EMAILS`: Merlin credits and admins
- `RN_INTEL_SEED_KEY` (opens `data/intel-seed.enc`), `RN_REQUIRE_SEED=1` (never run on the sample data: 503 everywhere and a failing health check if the seed does not open; set in the Dockerfile), `RN_ID_SECRET` (signs public ids and unsubscribe links: set it to the seed key's current value once and keep it)
- `RN_SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `RN_INTRO_EMAIL`: where members write for help and for an intro (`View.contact`, read at run time, never at build time)
- `RN_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` or `RN_TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS`: which address the rate limits and the invite rule read. Check once with `RN_LOG_IP_HEADERS=1` (logs the candidate headers of the first 20 requests), then remove it
- `RN_SCAN=1` or `RN_CRON_SECRET`: the weekly scan of open roles (see Weekly scan above). `RN_SCAN_TEST=1` is for the test suites only
- `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RN_EMAIL_FROM`, `RN_PUBLIC_URL`, `RN_DIGEST`, `RN_DIGEST_ALL`, `RN_EMAIL_FOOTER`, `RN_EMAIL_REPLY_TO` (the reply_to on every email, message emails included, so a reply reaches the team and never a member): one-time sign-in links and codes, message emails and the weekly email. `RN_EMAIL_API_URL` points email at a local mock in tests
- `RN_BACKUP_S3_ENDPOINT`, `RN_BACKUP_S3_BUCKET`, `RN_BACKUP_S3_KEY_ID`, `RN_BACKUP_S3_SECRET` (and `RN_BACKUP_S3_REGION`, `RN_BACKUP_S3_PATH_STYLE`): the off-site copy of the daily backup. `RN_LOGO_LOOKUP=off` stops logo lookups by name
- Health check: point the host at `GET /api/health` (200 only when the seed opened, the state loaded and the data folder takes writes). `railway.json` sets it with 15 seconds to drain; Railway may ignore that file, so set the path and the drain time in the service settings too
