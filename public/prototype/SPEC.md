# Revenue Nomad master prototype: product spec

Read with `DESIGN.md` (build contract). Research inventories live outside the repo; builders get their paths in the task.

## 1. What this is

One system for three audiences, built on one data model and one set of standard fields:

- **Clients** (companies hiring; never call them "buyers" in UI copy, L168) browse without login, shortlist, compare, request intros, post engagements, and keep their hires and terms in one workspace.
- **Operators** get a Studio that pays off even when no intro happens: who viewed and why, positioning, credibility for direct deals, search and AI visibility, opportunities.
- **Everyone** gets a destination for Fractional GTM: research, the Rate Index, the GTM Framework, the Fit Tag Library, guides, Engagement Blueprints.

The platform's own IP, surfaced everywhere and linked together: **Reputation Index** (0–100, tiers), **CORE** client reviews, **GTM Framework** (6 areas × 7 stages), **Fit Tag Library** (verified vs claimed), **Rate Index**, **State of Fractional GTM** research, **Engagement Blueprints** (scoped engagement templates), **Match Signals**.

### How Revenue Nomad is paid (founder decision D1, Sep 25, 2026)

Companies pay **no fees of any kind**: browsing, posting an engagement, intros and hiring are free to companies. A client pays only the operator's listed rate, to the operator. Revenue Nomad charges the **operator** a percentage of their billed earnings each month (proposed: 25%). That arrangement is behind the scenes between Revenue Nomad and operators.

- **Client-facing surfaces**: Home, Browse, Compare, operator profiles, proof pages and `#verify`, the intro sheet, the client workspace (`#buyer`), the client review, Engagements and Blueprints on the client side, Insights (hub, report, `#rates`, the calculator), Research (framework, library, guides), and company pages (`#how`, `#results`, `#about`, `#talk`, FAQ).
  - The operator's rate is the price: "$240/hr", "$9,000 - $13,500/mo". A label says "Rate", not "Operator rate", wherever "operator" only existed to contrast with an all-in price.
  - Where a page addresses pricing (Engagements and Blueprints, How it works, FAQ, Talk to us, the Rate Index intro, the intro sheet), say it positively and simply: "No fees for companies. You pay the operator's rate, nothing more."
  - Never mention a fee, margin, take rate, percentage, split, "all-in", "including the platform fee", "operator rate vs all-in" or take-home. Never say or imply that operators pay Revenue Nomad anything, receive less than their rate, or that a client pays more than the listed rate. Never "you pay only when you hire" or "every amount includes the fee".
  - No calculation adds a fee (no rate / 0.75, no × 1.333). A client budget ("Budget per hour") compares directly to rates, with no × 0.75 conversion.
- **Operator-facing surfaces** (Studio, `#operators`, `#levels`, `#join` and the operator intake, operator emails) state the fee plainly: "Revenue Nomad charges a percentage of your billed earnings each month (proposed: 25%). Clients pay your listed rate and no fees." Take-home (rate × 0.75) may appear there.
- **Admin** is internal and may keep fee and revenue metrics.

### Words (founder decision D12, Sep 25, 2026)

The thing a client posts is an **engagement**: "Post an Engagement", "Engagements", "engagement invites", "an engagement". "Project" stays only as an engagement type value (fixed scope and fee): Engagement type is Advisory, Interim, Fractional or Project. "Engagement history" on profiles is past work and keeps its name. Internal keys do not change (`RN.store.state.projects`, `RN.projects`, `projects.js`, CSS classes, event types such as `project_post`), so no data migration is needed.

## 3. The loops that make it one system (every builder must wire their side)

1. **Search → impressions → Studio "Why you appeared".** Browse logs `RN.track('search', {q, tags, filters, results})` once per search and `RN.track('impression', {opId, q, filters, position})` for the first 12 results. Studio Visibility reads them (plus illustrative history) through `RN.model.analytics`.
2. **Profile view → "Who viewed" (firmographic, never company names).** Profile logs `profile_view` with `source` (card, search, compare, proof, home, direct). When the viewer is the signed-in client, `RN.track` attaches their company firmographics automatically.
3. **Shortlist / compare → "Compared, not chosen".** `shortlist_add/remove`, `compare_add`, `compare_view` events feed Studio's lost-compare insights.
4. **Intro request → operator inbox → admin lifecycle → client workspace → hire.** One shared record (`RN.intro`), statuses Pending → Interested → RN Qualified → Introduced → Hired / Declined. Operators see requests blind (scope, value, firmographics) until Introduced. Moving an intro to Hired (the client's "Mark as hired", or Admin) opens "Confirm the terms" (`RN.hire.open`, loop 16).
5. **Engagement (from a Blueprint, key terms adjusted on the Blueprint page) → ranked matches → invites → operator responses → client selects → terms.** Same standard fields as operator profiles, so `RN.model.rank(brief)` needs no translation. Invites land in the Studio inbox. Select on a response opens `RN.hire.open`, which selects the response and staffs the engagement.
6. **Review request (Studio) → client review page → verified fit tags + Reputation Index.** Operators see only Sent / Completed. A submitted review verifies tags (rated 4.0+), adds to CORE and lifts the score; the profile updates immediately. The client's Team tab offers "Leave a review" once a hire ends, or any time after 30 days.
7. **Proof link (Studio) → prospect opens `#proof.<id>` → Studio shows who read what.** Direct-deal credibility: the prospect sees a notice that the operator can see what was read.
8. **Operator intake → pending → admin approves (Approve profile, Generate score) → profile live at Emerging 50.** The after-submit page explains the review timeline (comment H328).
9. **Zero-result and unmet searches → Admin demand view and Studio positioning.** "Clients searched for X, only N operators match" nudges operators to add or verify tags.
10. **Research ↔ marketplace.** Every chart in Insights uses the standard picklists, and every chart links into Browse with the same filter applied (for example, the Rate Index for Revenue Operations at $20M–$50M opens Browse with those filters).
11. **Proof link → lead.** A prospect on `#proof.<id>` books a call or asks for a reference with their email; that creates an intro record with `source:'proof'`, which shows in the Studio inbox as "From your proof link" and in Admin's clients by source. The owner's own views never count.
12. **Visitor → client.** A visitor who requests an intro becomes their own client (`RN.shell.setClient`, saved in `seen.client`); the workspace shows only their own records (matched on email). Jordan Ellis is the demo client (dock: "Switch client to the demo client"); the demo shortlist appears only for Jordan.
13. **Saved searches.** Clients save a Browse search in one click (`seen.savedSearches`, owner = client email), including location, time zone and US-hours filters; chips on Browse and a list in the workspace re-apply it; Admin counts `saved_search` events.
14. **Guide answers.** Operators answer a guide question with one engagement as proof (`state.answers`, in review); the team approves in Admin > Approvals; approved answers carry a byline on the guide (placement, never Reputation Index points).
15. **Requests in plain English.** A sentence typed in Home or Browse search ("We're a $12M SaaS company, about 80 people, based in Boston, need someone to build our sales team in two weeks, 2 days a week") is read by `RN.model.understand` into the standard fields: role category, industry, company revenue, employee range, location (country or US state), time zone, US hours, availability, minimum available time, engagement type, hourly rate cap (a monthly budget converts once hours are known), focus-area keywords and the need. Browse shows them as removable chips, says what it left out when nothing matches everything (location is dropped just before industries), and offers "Search the exact words instead". The need prefills the intro request; the sentence rides with the search event for Admin.
16. **Hire → terms, everywhere (founder decision D15, Sep 25, 2026).** `RN.hire.open({opId, source, sourceId, prefill})` (js/views/intro.js) is the one "Confirm the terms" modal, built from registry fields: engagement type, rate in $/hr (defaults to the operator's listed rate), available time (or project budget for a Project engagement type), start, term. Saving writes `RN.store.state.hires`, marks the source hired (intro status `hired`, or the engagement response selected and the engagement staffed), emails the client and the operator (`RN.mail`) and toasts a link to `#buyer.team`. The client's Team tab, the operator's Studio active engagements and Admin's hires list read the same record. `RN.hire.list(filter)`, `RN.hire.end(id)` and `RN.hire.extend(id, newTerm)` change it; `hire`, `hire_end` and `hire_extend` are tracked. No invented hires of real operators in seed data.

**Location filters (founder decision D9, Sep 25, 2026).** `RN.model.place(op)` returns `{city, state, country, tz}` (cached on `op.place`) from `op.location` and `op.timezone`: `state` is a US state's full name or empty, `country` is "United States" for US states and otherwise the normalized country ("UK" becomes "United Kingdom"), `tz` is one of `eastern, central, mountain, pacific, alaska_hawaii, uk_europe, other` (from `op.timezone`, else the US state, else the country). Registry: `F.locations` (Location, tag search, up to 5, "Country, or US state"; options filled at startup from the operators' places, countries first, then US states), `F.timeZones` (Time zone, chips) and `usHours` (reuses `RN.fields.usHours`; "yes" means based in the US or answered yes). Filter keys `filters.locations` (OR across countries and US states), `filters.timeZones` (OR) and `filters.usHours`, all AND with the other filters in `RN.model.search`. The drawer shows Location (tag search), Time zone (chips) and a "Works US hours" switch; active chips use the location type colour. `RN.model.understand` reads "based in <state, country or major city>", "in <state>", "US-based" or "in the US", "East Coast" (eastern), "West Coast" (pacific), "EST", "ET", "Eastern time", "Central time", "Pacific time", "UK", "London" and "Europe". Major cities map to states or countries: Boston → Massachusetts, New York or NYC → New York, SF, San Francisco, Bay Area, LA, Los Angeles or San Diego → California, Chicago → Illinois, Austin, Dallas or Houston → Texas, Denver → Colorado, Seattle → Washington, Atlanta → Georgia, Miami → Florida, Toronto → Canada, London → United Kingdom.

## 5. Decisions made in this prototype (for the founder to confirm)

| Conflict | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three Reputation Index ladders (sheet L247, explorer, live labels) and mismatched operator vs client labels (H390) | One ladder derived from the score everywhere: Indexing (<50, before approval) · Emerging 50–59 · Proven 60–69 · Trusted 70–79 · Elite 80–89 · Apex 90–100 | Consistent with the floor of 50; the explorer is the visual source of truth |
| "Verified" at score 50 for 78 of 100 live profiles with no client evidence | Confirmed: the entry tier is named "Emerging" (founder decision, Sep 25, 2026); "Verified" is reserved for client-confirmed proof (tags, engagements) | Keeps "Verified" credible, which the badge and proof links depend on |
| Tier badges looked alike, so a higher tier did not read as a better resource | One shared component, `RN.ui.tierBadge` and `RN.ui.tierLadder` (also used by `RN.ui.ris` and `RN.ui.hexSeal`); rarity reads from material, from a dashed outline (Indexing) to black stone in a gold rim (Apex). Never hand-draw a tier hexagon (founder decision D11, Sep 25, 2026) | Higher tiers look more valuable at a glance, in color and in grayscale |
| Who pays Revenue Nomad, and what clients see | Companies pay no fees of any kind; a client pays the operator's listed rate, to the operator. Revenue Nomad charges operators a percentage of their billed earnings each month (proposed: 25%). The fee is shown only on operator-facing and Admin surfaces; client pages never mention a fee, margin, split, all-in price or take-home (founder decision D1, Sep 25, 2026) | "The rates are the rates." The fee is behind the scenes between Revenue Nomad and operators, never an added cost to companies |
| "Project" for something that is often a fractional role | What a client posts is an **engagement**; "Project" is only the engagement type value (fixed scope and fee). Routes `#engagements`, `#engagement.new`, `#engagement.<id>`; old `#projects` and `#project.*` links redirect; internal keys unchanged (founder decision D12, Sep 25, 2026) | One word that fits advisory, interim, fractional and project work, and matches Engagement Blueprints |
| Editing a Blueprint before posting | Key terms (engagement type, available time, term, start) edit inline on the Blueprint page; "Customize every detail" opens the full form prefilled at its first step; the review step has an Edit link per section (founder decision D3, Sep 25, 2026) | Clients adjust a template without losing the 5-click fast path |
| Blueprint cards were bland and text-heavy | Problem headline as the hero, category-hue accent and icon, 30/60/90-day mini timeline, meta chips, one "Start from this" action, about 25 words at most (founder decision D13, Sep 25, 2026) | The grid scans at a glance; the detail lives on the Blueprint page |
| Fractional vs full-time calculator ignored variable pay | The full-time side adds target commission and bonus (defaults by role category), the recruiting fee applies to first-year cash pay, and the fractional side is the rate itself (founder decision D2, Sep 25, 2026) | Commission and bonus are a real part of a full-time GTM hire's cost |
| Comparing operators in different role categories | Shared questions side by side; a Comparable scope section maps role answers onto shared measures with the source question shown; the rest grouped by role, with "Not asked for {role}" apart from "Not added" (founder decision D8, Sep 25, 2026) | Roles answer different onboarding questions; a question never asked is not a blank |
| Location was not a filter | Location (country or US state), time zone and "Works US hours" filters, read from one normalized place per operator (`RN.model.place`) and from plain-English requests (founder decision D9, Sep 25, 2026) | Clients often need someone in their region or working their hours |
| Home spoke to clients and operators | Home is for companies hiring; operator messaging (Studio preview, how featuring works, join calls to action) lives on `#operators`, with one client-facing featuring line on Home (founder decision D7, Sep 25, 2026) | One audience per page; the header link and footer reach operators |
| After a hire, the client had nowhere to see who they hired or on what terms | A confirmed hire records its terms (`RN.store.state.hires`, `RN.hire.open`) and shows in the workspace Team tab, the operator's Studio and Admin (founder decision D15, Sep 25, 2026) | The client's record of their fractional team, with extend, end and review in one place |
| Intro start timeline (ASAP / 2 Weeks / 1+ Month) vs availability chips | Client start timeline uses the availability slugs | Matches operator availability with no translation |
| Review form Project / Retainer and LinkedIn size bands | Review form uses the four engagement types and the standard employee ranges | One vocabulary across intake, intros, engagements and reviews |
| Hours stored as raw numbers (legacy 5/10/15/30) | Stored as the chip codes (19 = <20, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 160) | "Store chip keys, not numbers" |
| Tool proficiency "Builder" vs "Power User" | Power User (intake and comment E397) | Latest founder wording |
| Fit tag filter OR vs AND | AND for focus areas (up to 5), OR within role categories, industries, locations and time zones | Part A / Part B final rows |
| GTM motion 4 vs 5 options ("Direct") | Four: PLG, Channel, Inside Sales, Enterprise Sales | "Direct" was never confirmed |
| Who viewed | Firmographic segments only, groups under 5 visits combined; never company or person names | Privacy and trust; Performance & Insights spec |
| A visitor requesting an intro vs the demo client | The visitor becomes their own client account (their company, their records only); Jordan Ellis stays the demo client | A new client never sees another company's data |
| Demo review request at a real company (Trialbee) | Placeholder sponsor with an example address; the reviewer types their own name | Never attach invented people to real companies; founder to supply the real contact |
| Proof-page "Book a call" / "Request a reference" | Creates an intro with `source:'proof'` in the operator's inbox, and emails both sides | Direct-deal credibility turns into a trackable lead |
| Proof links by tier | Emerging: 5 a month; Proven and above: unlimited plus the embeddable badge (proposed) | Credibility for direct deals from day one, more as you invest |
| Newsletter | One list, The Fractional GTM Pulse, **quarterly**; the next issue is the first day of the next calendar quarter; Home and Insights share it (founder decision D6, Sep 25, 2026) | Not enough new research for a weekly issue; one destination, one voice |
| Founder on the platform | No founder callouts; the founder's operator profile follows the same rules as every operator (founder decision, Sep 25, 2026). No founder pill, line or disclosure on cards, profile, proof page, `#verify`, Home, Studio previews or engagement invites; no exclusions from featured, strong-fit, "strongest here", related or similar lists. `op.isMatt` drives demo data only. The About page keeps the company story, founder note and photo | Even playing field |
| Role details | The live signup's full field set per category, including CS GRR, team size, largest account and ownership model, Marketing team size and Partnerships ecosystem; picklists and caps match the Scope sheet; CRM asked of every role | Standard fields everywhere, so intake, profile, compare and admin read the same keys |
